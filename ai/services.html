AI Email Automation

Agentic LLM pipeline for email triage

AI email automation uses an LLM to read each incoming message, classify intent, retrieve relevant data from a CRM or ERP via tool-calling, and generate a structured reply for human review or direct sending. Built with FastAPI, embeddings, and a prompted or fine-tuned model.

Document AI & RAG Chatbots

Private RAG systems for document question answering

A RAG chatbot retrieves relevant passages from private documents at question time, inserts those passages into the prompt, and asks the LLM to answer using the retrieved evidence. I design RAG systems with chunking, embeddings, vector databases, retrieval evaluation, citations, and guardrails.

Who this is for

Law firms, HR teams, customer support teams, internal operations, and SMEs with private document collections.

What I deliver

Document ingestion pipeline, embedding and vector store setup, retrieval-augmented answer generation, source traceability, and deployment.
