Productized AI Systems

Reusable implementation packages for teams that need production AI workflows faster than a fully custom build: email triage, private RAG, manufacturing BI, meeting intelligence, and full-stack AI applications.

Why productized systems?

Most AI projects fail because teams start from a blank page. These packages start from proven architecture patterns, then adapt the data model, model choices, integrations, and user interface to the role or business workflow.
